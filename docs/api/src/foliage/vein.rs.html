11/// Exactly what a frame may read of an element.
12///
13/// Exhaustive by construction: if it is not here, an app cannot see it. Everything an app can
14/// declare is here, because a value you can set and cannot read back is a value you have to keep a
15/// copy of.
16#[derive(Copy, Clone, PartialEq, Eq, Hash, Debug)]
17#[non_exhaustive]
18pub enum Vein {
19    /// The elements branched directly off this one: what the app grew there, and never the parts
20    /// an element like a [`TextInput`](crate::TextInput) grows under itself.
21    Branches,
22    /// The element this one was branched off, if any.
23    Trunk,
24    /// Where the layout put the element, before any scrolling or [shift](Vein::Shift) moved it.
25    ///
26    /// What a containing view's extent is measured from, and what to read when the question is
27    /// where content sits rather than where it currently appears.
28    Placed,
29    /// The element's box less every scrolling ancestor's offset, and moved by every shift on it and
30    /// above it: where it is on screen, which is what was drawn and what a hit test runs against.
31    ///
32    /// The two differ only inside a view that has been scrolled, or under a shift.
33    Drawn,
34    /// The one other element this one's placement may read, if it has been given one.
35    Anchor,
36    /// How far in front of its trunk the element was told to sit.
37    ///
38    /// What was declared, not where it ended up: the resolved value accumulates its whole ancestry
39    /// and carries a tie-break, and neither is a number an app has any use for.
40    Elevation,
41    /// What the element is filled with, if it is something with a fill: the role it declared, or
42    /// the color it named outright.
43    ///
44    /// What was declared, not what it currently paints as. A role's color is the
45    /// [`Scheme`](crate::Scheme)'s answer and is resolved at extraction, so there is no resolved
46    /// color held anywhere for this to report.
47    Color,
48    /// Where a stroke's two ends landed, which is what [`Drawn`](Vein::Drawn) cannot say.
49    ///
50    /// A box is the rectangle around them grown by half the weight, and a rectangle has two
51    /// diagonals -- so the box says how much room the stroke takes and this says where it runs.
52    /// `None` on anything placed by a box.
53    ///
54    /// Resolved rather than declared, on the same terms as `Drawn`: the two ends as the layout
55    /// answered them and scrolling moved them, not the grammar they were written in.
56    Ends,
57    /// How thick a stroke is drawn, in logical pixels. `None` on anything that is not a stroke.
58    Weight,
59    /// How a stroke's ends are finished.
60    Cap,
61    /// What a regular polygon looks like: its sides, its rounding and its rotation.
62    ///
63    /// While a [`Motion::Polygon`](crate::Motion::Polygon) is running this is where it has reached,
64    /// because a blend of two shapes is a shape and is written back over the declaration -- the same
65    /// standing [`Opacity`](Vein::Opacity) has.
66    Shape,
67    /// Which registered mark an icon draws.
68    Mark,
69    /// Which registered picture an image draws.
70    ///
71    /// The name only. Whether its pixels have arrived is not readable and is deliberately not a
72    /// question an app has to ask: an element drawing a picture that has not loaded occupies its box
73    /// and draws nothing, and appears when the pixels do.
74    Picture,
75    /// How an image's pixels are fitted into its box.
76    Fit,
77    /// How the element's corners are rounded, if it is something with corners.
78    Rounding,
79    /// The line drawn around the inside of a [`Panel`](crate::Panel): how wide, and what it is
80    /// filled with. A width of zero where it has none. `None` on anything that is not a panel.
81    Outline,
82    /// What the element says, if it is a run of glyphs.
83    ///
84    /// What was written, not how it wrapped: where the lines fell is a function of the box the
85    /// layout gave it, and the box is [`Drawn`](Vein::Drawn).
86    Text,
87    /// What a [`TextInput`](crate::TextInput) or a [`TextArea`](crate::TextArea) has selected, in
88    /// characters of its value.
89    ///
90    /// Empty where nothing is selected, and its one end is then where the caret is -- so a caret
91    /// and a selection are one read rather than two that could disagree.
92    /// `None` on anything that is not a field.
93    Selection,
94    /// Whether the app has hidden the element.
95    ///
96    /// What was declared of this element, not the product over its ancestry: an element inside a
97    /// hidden subtree reads visible, because that is what it says about itself and what showing its
98    /// ancestor again would leave it as.
99    Visible,
100    /// How opaque the element was told to be, on the same terms as [`Visible`](Vein::Visible).
101    ///
102    /// While a [`Motion::Opacity`](crate::Motion::Opacity) is running this is where it has reached,
103    /// because a blend of two numbers is a number and is written back over the declaration. That is
104    /// what is on screen, which is what a read is for.
105    Opacity,
106    /// Whether the element was disabled in its own right, on the same terms as
107    /// [`Visible`](Vein::Visible).
108    Disabled,
109    /// How far the element was [shifted](crate::Grow::shift) from where the layout put it, in
110    /// logical pixels -- its own, not what it was moved by from above, on the same terms as
111    /// [`Visible`](Vein::Visible). Zero where it never was.
112    ///
113    /// While a [`Motion::Shift`](crate::Motion::Shift) is running this is where it has reached, as
114    /// with [`Opacity`](Vein::Opacity).
115    Shift,
116    /// How far a scrolling region has been moved from its content's origin, in logical pixels.
117    ///
118    /// The same unit it is written in, so reading it back after a
119    /// [`scroll`](crate::Grow::scroll) returns the pixels it settled at. `None` on anything that
120    /// does not scroll: an element with no scrolling axis has no offset, rather than an offset of
121    /// zero it can never leave.
122    Offset,
123    /// How far a scrolling region's content reaches, from its own near edges.
124    ///
125    /// What a scrollbar's thumb is a fraction of. An axis the region does not scroll reads the
126    /// region's own box, which is the same statement as having no extent on it.
127    Extent,
128    /// How far through its range a scrolling region sits, per axis, in `0.0..=1.0`.
129    ///
130    /// Derived from the two above rather than held, and read-only for that reason: pixels are the
131    /// one unit an offset is written in. A region with nowhere to go reads zero on that axis.
132    Progress,
133}

135/// What a [`Vein`] draws out.
136#[derive(Clone, PartialEq, Debug)]
137#[non_exhaustive]
138pub enum Sap {
139    /// A set of elements, in the order the tree holds them. What [`Branches`](Vein::Branches)
140    /// answers.
141    Leaves(Vec<Leaf>),
142    /// One element, or none where there is nothing to name. What [`Trunk`](Vein::Trunk) and
143    /// [`Anchor`](Vein::Anchor) answer.
144    Leaf(Option<Leaf>),
145    /// A box. What [`Placed`](Vein::Placed) and [`Drawn`](Vein::Drawn) answer.
146    Section(Section),
147    /// Logical pixels, like every other coordinate.
148    Position(Position),
149    /// An extent, in logical pixels.
150    Area(Area),
151    /// A fraction on each axis, in `0.0..=1.0`. Held apart from a [`Position`](Sap::Position) even
152    /// though it is the same pair of numbers, so a read cannot quietly take a fraction for pixels.
153    Progress(Position),
154    /// How far in front of its trunk an element was told to sit.
155    Elevation(Elevation),
156    /// What an element is filled with: the role it declared, or the color it named outright.
157    Color(Fill),
158    /// The radius bracket on each of the four corners.
159    Rounding(Corners),
160    /// How wide a panel's outline is, in logical pixels, and what it is filled with.
161    Outline(f32, Fill),
162    /// A stroke's two ends, in the order they were written.
163    Ends(Position, Position),
164    /// A stroke's thickness, in logical pixels.
165    Weight(f32),
166    /// How a stroke's ends are finished.
167    Cap(Cap),
168    /// A regular polygon's sides, rounding and rotation.
169    Shape(Shape),
170    /// Which registered mark an icon draws.
171    Mark(Field),
172    /// Which registered picture an image draws.
173    Picture(Plate),
174    /// How an image's pixels are fitted into its box.
175    Fit(Fit),
176    /// What a run of glyphs says.
177    Text(String),
178    /// A span of a value, in characters. Empty means a caret at its own position.
179    Selection(core::ops::Range<usize>),
180    /// Whether the app has hidden the element.
181    Visible(bool),
182    /// How opaque the element was told to be, in `0.0..=1.0`.
183    Opacity(f32),
184    /// Whether the element was disabled in its own right.
185    Disabled(bool),
186}
