1//! Cross-platform, ECS-backed UI.
2//!
3//! Everything crossing the seam between an app and the engine is plain data: ops in, emissions
4//! out, and read-only taps taken at the app's own callsite. No engine type is handed out and
5//! nothing an app holds borrows from the world.
6//!
7//! - [`Foliage`] is the engine. [`Grove`] is the surface it hands a frame, and [`Root`] is the app
8//!   it hands it to.
9//! - [`Leaf`] names an element and [`Seed`] describes one before it exists. [`Grow`] carries every
10//!   write, [`Pollen`] is what comes back out.
11//! - [`Vein`] asks a read for one property; [`Sap`] is what the tap draws out.
12//! - [`Sprig`] is the engine reached from off the frame -- every write, every registration, and the
13//!   reads a thread that cannot sample needs: [`Conditions`] and a [`watch`](Sprig::watch).
14//! - [`Place`] states where an element sits and how it behaves, and [`Location`] carries the
15//!   grammar the first of those is said in.
16//! - [`Panel`] is a filled rectangle; [`Fill`] is what fills it -- a [`Palette`] role or a [`Color`]
17//!   stated outright -- and [`Corners`] is how it is rounded.
18//! - A gesture goes to the top of the box stack: [`interactive`](Place::interactive) says who
19//!   receives one, [`intangible`](Place::intangible) says who it passes through, and
20//!   [`Drag`] is what one reports while it is held. A press that stayed still for [`Hold`]'s
21//!   duration is [`held`](Pollen::held) rather than a tap that might still be coming.
22//! - [`Font`], [`Field`] and [`Plate`] are registered from bytes an app holds or from an [`Origin`]
23//!   to read them at, and [`loaded`](Pollen::loaded) says when one arrived.
24//! - A [`Keystroke`] goes to whatever holds focus, which is only ever something that declared
25//!   [`interactive`](Place::interactive): [`keys`](Pollen::keys) is what an element was sent, and
26//!   [`root_keys`](Pollen::root_keys) is what arrived with focus nowhere.
27//! - [`Text`] is a run of monospaced glyphs, [`Font`] names one an app registered and [`FontSize`]
28//!   says how large it is; [`content()`] is a box measured rather than declared.
29//! - [`TextInput`] is an editable one: one [`Leaf`] to hold, [`select`](Grow::select) to move its
30//!   caret, and [`edited`](Pollen::edited) and [`submitted`](Pollen::submitted) to hear what was
31//!   typed into it. It answers `Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+X` and `Ctrl+V` itself, and raises the [`Keypad`] it
32//!   named on a platform that has a keyboard to raise.
33//! - The host's own: [`copy`](Grow::copy) and [`paste`](Grow::paste) reach the clipboard, with
34//!   [`pasted`](Pollen::pasted) carrying what came back, and [`navigate`](Grow::navigate) and
35//!   [`download`](Grow::download) hand it a URL.
36//! - [`Motion`] is what can be animated and [`Timing`] how long it takes; [`Ease`] is the shape it
37//!   moves in, [`Tween`] names a value the engine only reports, and [`Sequence`] names a group of
38//!   them arriving.
39//! - An element scrolls because it said so: [`scrolls`](Place::scrolls) declares which axes and
40//!   whether each [`Scroll`]s outward or contains, [`pinned`](Place::pinned) keeps a child out of
41//!   the movement, [`ScrollTo`] is how a region is moved by name, and [`Momentum`] is how a release
42//!   coasts.
